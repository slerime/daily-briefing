Singapore tightens monetary policy in surprise move as rising oil prices rekindle inflation risk

Singapore's Monetary Authority surprised markets by tightening monetary policy as rising oil prices rekindled inflation risks. Unlike most central banks, the MAS manages price stability through the Singapore dollar exchange rate against a trade-weighted basket of currencies. This approach allows Singapore to control medium-term inflation without traditional interest rate tools.
