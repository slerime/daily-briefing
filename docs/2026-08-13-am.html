U.S. budget deficit surged in July to highest level since March 2021

The U.S. budget deficit reached its highest level since March 2021 in July, with a significant monthly jump. Over the first 10 months of the fiscal year, the cumulative deficit has risen to nearly $1.8 trillion, exceeding the same period in the previous year.
