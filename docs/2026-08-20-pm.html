Nvidia plays matchmaker in Nordics, sources tell CNBC, as AI data center deals boom in region

Nvidia is acting as a matchmaker between GPU customers and Nordic data-center operators as the region experiences an AI infrastructure boom. Cheap electricity and available land in the Nordic region are attracting major AI infrastructure investment. Nvidia's role in facilitating these connections underscores the company's expanding influence in the global AI ecosystem.
